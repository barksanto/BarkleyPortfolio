When someone needs what you offer, they search for it. If your business doesn't show up, that customer goes to whoever does. SEO (search engine optimization) is the work of making sure Google understands your business, trusts your website and shows it to the people most likely to become your customers.

I focus on the fundamentals that matter most for small and mid-sized businesses: a technically sound website, a strong Google Business Profile, pages that match what your customers actually type into Google, and a site fast enough that people stick around.

You won't get jargon-filled reports or vague promises. You'll get clear priorities, real fixes and an honest picture of where you stand and what it will take to grow.
