AI can save a small business hours every week, but it's also easy to spend money on tools that sound impressive and don't fit how you actually work. The goal isn't to add AI everywhere. It's to find the few places where it removes busywork or makes life easier for your customers.

Through my work with DataAnnotation, I train and evaluate AI models, so I see first-hand what today's AI does well and where it still falls short. I use that experience to recommend and build AI features that are reliable, affordable and genuinely useful, and to tell you honestly when a simpler solution would work better.

Whether it's automating how new leads are handled, speeding up content creation or adding smart features to your website, you'll get practical AI that fits your business.
